Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sanesy?

Sanesy is an academic social network where students track goals, study together, and use evidence-based learning tools in one distraction-free workspace.

How is Sanesy different from typical social media?

There are no engagement-bait algorithms or ads. Your feed surfaces academic progress, peer commitments, and study sessions.

Is Sanesy free to use for students?

Core features are free for verified students. Optional premium tools will be listed on the pricing page.

Can I use Sanesy with my existing study group?

Yes. Invite your group, set shared milestones, and hold each other accountable in a private circle or virtual room.
